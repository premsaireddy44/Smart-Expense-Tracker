Privacy and data deletion

Smart Expense Tracker is a personal portfolio project. Please do not enter anything you would not want a demo project to hold. This page explains, in plain words, what the app stores and how to get it erased.

What we store

  • Your email address.
  • Your password, stored only as a scrambled hash. Nobody, including the owner, can read it.
  • The transactions, budgets and savings goal you add.

What we do not store

  • No bank, card or UPI details. The app never asks for them.
  • No advertising or tracking cookies, and no analytics.

Your login on this device

When you log in, a login token is saved in your browser so you stay logged in. Logging out removes it.

Age

The app is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a young person has signed up, write to the contact below and the account will be erased.

Delete your data

The fastest way: log in, scroll to the bottom of the dashboard, choose Delete my account and data, and enter your password. Your account, transactions, budgets and savings goal are erased straight away. This cannot be undone.

If you cannot log in: email the contact below from the address you registered with and ask for deletion. Your data will be erased within 30 days.
