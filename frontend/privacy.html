Privacy notice

Smart Expense Tracker is a learning and portfolio project. This notice says plainly what it does with your data.

What is stored

  • Your email address and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password. We never store the password itself.
  • The transactions, budgets and savings goal you enter, including the descriptions you type.
  • For each transaction: which category was suggested, and whether you kept it or changed it. This is used to show you how well the suggestions work.

How categories are suggested

A small machine-learning model runs on the same server as the app. Your descriptions are not sent to any outside company or service. The app also remembers the categories you chose before, and that memory is private to your account.

What is not done

  • No advertising, no tracking scripts, no data sold or shared.
  • No cookies. Your login token is kept in your browser tab only and is forgotten when you close the tab.

Your control

Go to Account → Delete my account to erase your account and everything stored for it, immediately and permanently.

Age

You must be 16 or older to create an account.

Not financial advice

The numbers, insights and estimates are simple calculations on what you entered. They are not financial advice.
